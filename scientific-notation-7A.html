2 · EXPLANATION

Move the decimal point

Scientific notation is a shorthand method for writing large numbers. Try it: choose a number from the book, then move the decimal point left until the first part is at least 1 and less than 10.

700 000 = 7 × 105moved 5 places
8 296 000 = 8.296 × 106moved 6 places
58 000 = 5.8 × 104moved 4 places

Think and Discuss

Think first, then tap to see a model answer. Purple = my own example, not from the book.

Check two things: the first part must be at least 1 and less than 10, and the second part must be a power of 10.
The first part, 782.5, is greater than 10. To fix it, write 782.5 as 7.825 × 102, so 782.5 × 108 = 7.825 × 1010.
Advantages: it is shorter, you write fewer zeros (so it is easier not to lose count of them), and you can compare big numbers by looking at the powers of 10. Disadvantage: you must change it back to standard form to see the full number.

    4 · SUMMARY

    What to remember

    • A number in scientific notation has two parts that are multiplied: a number that is at least 1 and less than 10, and a power of 10. 3.456 × 106
    • To write a large number in scientific notation: move the decimal point left to form a number from 1 up to 10, then multiply by a power of ten. The power is the number of places you moved. 58 000 = 5.8 × 104
    • To go back to standard form: look at the power of 10 and move the decimal point that many places to the right. 3.2 × 107 = 32 000 000
    • Check yourself: is the first part at least 1 and less than 10? Is the second part a power of 10? 56.32 × 106 fails the first test.
