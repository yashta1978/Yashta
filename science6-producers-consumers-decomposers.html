Learn the definitions

Producer

An organism that can make its own food by using energy from its surrounding.

Easy example: grass.

Consumer

An organism that cannot make its own food therefore eats other organisms or organic matter.

Easy example: a rabbit eats grass, so a rabbit is a consumer.

Decomposers

An organism that breaks down the remains of dead organisms or animal wastes to obtain energy and nutrients.

Easy example: a mushroom.
