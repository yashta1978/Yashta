5. Pablo Picasso

Pablo Picasso is one of the most prominent and influential painters and sculptors of the 20th century, recognized as a pioneer of modern art.

Born in 1881 in the city of Málaga, Spain.

His artistic life passed through several distinct periods, including:

  • Blue Period: dominated mostly by shades of blue and themes of sadness.
  • Rose Period: used warmer colors and brighter scenes.
  • Cubism: in collaboration with Georges Braque, he co-founded the Cubist movement, which revolutionized the art world by breaking down shapes into triangles, squares, and geometric lines.

His most famous work: the masterpiece "Guernica", which is a powerful expression against war and destruction.

Passed away in 1973 in France at the age of 91, leaving behind a legacy of thousands of remarkable artworks.
