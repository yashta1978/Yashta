Summary

  • A power whose exponent is 2 is called a square: the area of a square with side 3 is 3 × 3 = 3².
  • A perfect square is the square of a natural number (36 = 6²).
  • The square root of a number is one of its two equal factors: √16 = 4 because 4 × 4 = 16. √ is the radical sign.
  • To estimate √n: find the nearest perfect squares, take their square roots, see which is closer, then check with a calculator.
