Art 7A – Art and Colors

Lesson goal

By the end of this lesson you can:

  • Say the definition of Art and of Painting.
  • Name the kinds of Fine Art.
  • Sort colors into primary and secondary, and into hot and cold.
  • Mix two primary colors and name the new color.

Summary

  • Art = any work a person does and makes progress in. Art is passion and innovation: theatre, music, painting.
  • Painting: a picture is a book of nature we read with our feelings and conscience.
  • Fine Art: plastic art, calligraphy, sculpture, ceramics, design.
  • Primary: red, yellow, blue. Secondary: green, orange, purple. Hot: red, yellow, orange. Cold: blue, purple.
  • Mixing: red + blue = purple; yellow + red = orange; blue + yellow = green.
