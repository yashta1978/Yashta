PE 6A – Football

Lesson goal

By the end of this lesson you can:

  • Give the numbers of football: players, starting players, substitutions.
  • Say the safety rules and name the player equipment.
  • Give the shape and dimensions of the football pitch.
  • Give the dimensions of the goal.

1. Number of players

Players: a team consists of a total of (24) players, and the match is played between two teams where each team has (11) players playing, and there is a goalkeeper.

Start of the match: each team must start with at least (7) players; if there are fewer players, the match cannot take place. Each team is allowed (5) substitutions, no more.

2. Player safety

Players must protect themselves so as not to get hurt.

Players are not allowed to wear jewelry or anything that poses a danger to them or other players.

For example: necklace, bracelet, watch, earring, ring… etc.

Player equipment

  1. Jersey
  2. Shorts
  3. Shin guards
  4. Socks
  5. Footwear (Cleats)
  6. The shorts and jersey must be clean.

3. Shape and dimensions of the football pitch

  1. The football pitch must be rectangular.
  2. Length of the pitch: 90–120 m
  3. Width of the pitch: 45–90 m
  4. Width of the pitch lines: 12 cm
  5. Center circle radius: 9.15 m
  6. Penalty area / penalty spot distance: 11 m

Correction: the printed page says width "45–75 m". It was corrected on the page to 45–90 m, and the pitch picture also says 45–90 m. Learn 45–90 m.

4. The goal

  1. Length of the goal: 7.32 m
  2. Height of the goal: 2.44 m
  3. Thickness of the posts and crossbar: 12 cm, like all other pitch lines (12 cm).

Summary

  • Players: 24 in a team; 11 play from each team, with a goalkeeper.
  • Start: at least 7 players; 5 substitutions, no more.
  • Safety: no jewelry (necklace, bracelet, watch, earring, ring).
  • Equipment: jersey, shorts, shin guards, socks, cleats; shorts and jersey clean.
  • Pitch: rectangular; length 90–120 m; width 45–90 m; lines 12 cm; center circle radius 9.15 m; penalty spot 11 m.
  • Goal: length 7.32 m; height 2.44 m; posts and crossbar 12 cm.
