I Discover

A football stadium holds more than 100,000 spectators. How do you use exponents to write 100,000?

100,000 = 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 × 10. The factor 10 is repeated 5 times, so we write 100,000 = 105. The next section shows this step by step.

I Learn

Power: Any number that is raised to an exponent is called a power of that number. Therefore, a power consists of base and exponent.

  • I write large numbers in exponential form.
  • Exponents make large numbers shorter and easier to express and write.

Base: The factor that is repeated (the number being multiplied repeatedly).

Exponent: The number of times the base is repeated.

Remember: Writing in exponential form is an appropriate way to write repeated multiplication of the same factor in a compact form.

Factor: Numbers that are multiplied together to obtain another number.

Be careful:

Common mistake: 105 = 10 × 5 = 50 (wrong)

The correct way: 105 = 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 × 10 = 100000 (correct)

The exponent tells how many times to write the base. It is never multiplied by the base.

Vocabulary

Summary

  1. A power has a base and an exponent: in 42 the base is 4 and the exponent is 2.
  2. The exponent tells how many times the base is written as a factor: 42 = 4 × 4 = 16.
  3. Never multiply the base by the exponent: 25 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 = 32, not 2 × 5.
  4. The same number can be written with different bases: 81 = 34 = 92.
  5. Special powers: a1 = a, a0 = 1 (a is not zero), and 1 to any power is 1.
